Kakwe privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. Kakwe helps people check job offers, messages and links for scams and trafficking. This page lists what we store and why.

Kakwe is for people aged 18 and older.

Using Kakwe without an account

Each install gets an anonymous Firebase ID (a random uid). We use it only to limit how many checks one install can run per hour. Your check history and settings stay on your device. You cannot save new trusted contacts without an account. Contacts you saved on your device before this change stay there and you can still use them to warn someone.

If you create an account

You can sign up with an email and password, or with Google or Facebook. We then store your email address and sign-in provider (in Firebase Authentication), and we sync three things to our database (Firebase Firestore, region asia-southeast1):

  • a short record of each check: the verdict, a score range, the scam type, the report title and summary that Kakwe wrote, the language, the kind of check and the date. We do not sync your message, the screenshot, links, quotes from your message or source pages. Records synced by older versions of the app held the full report; the app rewrites them in this short form the next time you sign in;
  • your trusted contacts (name and phone number you typed or picked). Saving a trusted contact needs a signed-in account, and contacts you saved on your device earlier are synced to your account when you sign in;
  • your profile (language, home country, destination country).

Only you can read this data. It is protected by security rules that allow each signed-in user to access only their own documents. Anonymous installs cannot read or write any of it.

What happens to what you check

To check something, the app sends the text, link or screenshot to our check service. The service uses Google Gemini models, Google Search grounding, Google Web Risk and public domain records (RDAP) to assess it. We do not log the content of your checks. Screenshots are processed in memory and are not saved.

The Google Search step receives only the names of companies or agencies and the website addresses found in the check, plus the country. It never receives people's names, phone numbers, chat ids or account numbers.

Cloudflare carries the app's traffic to our check service. Cloudflare can see this traffic while it is in transit.

Check history on your phone

Your checks are saved on your phone so you can open them again. In Settings you can turn off "Keep check history on this phone". The app then deletes the saved checks, keeps only the check you are looking at, and, if you are signed in, deletes the synced records and stops syncing checks.

On the Check, Report and Get help screens, "Leave quickly" goes straight to the Home screen and closes the report and any spoken reply. In a web browser, the tab title shows only the name Kakwe.

The app keeps a diagnostics log on your phone (Settings, "Diagnostics log") that holds error codes and timings but no message content or personal details, and it is shared only if you choose to send it.

Anonymous scam signals (optional, off by default)

Sharing is off until you say yes. The app asks once, and you can change the answer at any time in Settings under "Help others: share anonymous scam patterns". When it is on, a check that looks risky can add an anonymous signal to a shared scam radar. A signal holds the scam type, verdict, score range, flag types, country and language, and a short rewritten template of the message with names and personal details removed. Phone numbers, chat ids and account numbers are stored only as one-way codes that cannot be turned back into the number. Website names are stored as they are, because they are public. A signal holds no screenshot, no copy of your message and no name or email. A reporter key made from a one-way hash of your uid and the date is used to count each person once per day. Signals are deleted after 90 days. Checks made while sharing is off send no signal.

"Report a scam" shares a signal even when sharing is off, because you choose to send that report. The app says so on that screen.

Who we share data with

We use Google Firebase and Google Cloud to run the service, and Cloudflare to carry traffic to it. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, that provider gives us your name, email and a sign-in token. We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

Your choices

  • Delete the data on your device: Settings, "Delete data on this device".
  • Delete your account and everything synced to it: Settings, "Delete account". See how to delete your data.
  • Turn anonymous signals on or off: Settings, "Help others: share anonymous scam patterns".
  • Stop saving checks on your phone: Settings, "Keep check history on this phone".
