Delete your SafeRoute data

You can delete your data yourself in the app. This also covers anyone who signed in with Google or Facebook.

Delete your account and synced data

  1. Open SafeRoute and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear icon on Home to open Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your sign-in, your synced check history, trusted contacts and profile, and clears the data on that device. If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign in and repeat the steps.

Delete only the data on your device

Settings, then Delete data on this device. If you are signed in, this also signs you out. Your account and its synced copies stay until you delete the account.

Anonymous scam signals

Anonymous signals contain no name, email or uid, so they cannot be linked back to you or removed one by one. They expire automatically after 90 days. To stop contributing, turn off "Help others: share anonymous scam patterns" in Settings.

Cannot open the app?

Email support@akkhayar.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account and synced data.
