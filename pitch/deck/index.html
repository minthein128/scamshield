Responsible AI

Safety and privacy

Likelihood words

"Shows signs of", never "is a scam".

No accusations

No named person is called a criminal.

Consent first

Sharing and Message Guard are off until she turns them on.

Hashed indicators

Leave quickly

History keeps the verdict and summary only.
